Objective

Rotate the neural conduits to establish continuous pathways from Action Potential Somata (Sources) to all Synaptic Receptors (Targets).

Controls

  • Left-Click / Tap: Rotate tile 90° clockwise.
  • Right-Click: Rotate tile 90° counter-clockwise.
  • WASD / Arrow Keys: Move grid selection cursor.
  • Space / Enter: Rotate selected tile.
  • U / Ctrl+Z: Undo last turn.
  • R: Reset puzzle.
  • H: Intelligent Hint.

Synaptic Components

  • Quantum Bridge: Transmits two signals right across each other without collision.
  • Logic AND Gate (&): Only fires an output signal once TWO incoming active signals cross its threshold simultaneously!
  • Frequency Transmuter: Shifts the signal frequency (e.g. from Cyan to Magenta) to satisfy multi-channel receptors.
